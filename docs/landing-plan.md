One offer: a guided arrival in Dubai. One audience: international students (and, as a second reader, their universities). One primary action: **Open the app**.

## 2. Hero copy
- Headline: "Feel at home" + highlight pill "before you land."
- Sub: "Wayfare is one trusted, guided journey for international students arriving in Dubai. Verified housing, an offline transit map and real costs in your home currency, ready before Day Zero."
- CTA: Open the app
- Proof: "42,026 students now study in Dubai. 35% are international. Source: KHDA, 2024–25."

## 3. Benefits
- **Verified places to live**: your university checks the price, deposit and source before the badge shows.
- **A transit map that works offline**: route DXB Terminal 1 to campus with no data on Day Zero.
- **Real costs in your currency**: AED and rupees side by side, with the rate date. We never guess your rent.
- **A checklist that understands the Emirates ID gap**: see what works with your passport today and what unlocks later.
- **One link for your family**: a read only budget and plan, no login.

## 4. How it works
1. Prepare (before departure): housing, documents, budget, offline map.
2. Arrive (Day Zero to Day 6): airport to campus, SIM and transport card, medical and biometrics.
3. Settle (week 1 onwards): Emirates ID unlocks, banking, Ejari, community.

## 5. FAQ
- Who verifies listings? The university's housing office, in the Wayfare portal, with a price list or agreement as the source.
- Does the transit map work offline? Yes. It is saved on your phone the first time you open the app.
- Do I need an Emirates ID to use Wayfare? No. Most of Day Zero works with your passport.
- Which currencies can I see? AED plus about 30 home currencies, using daily rates from Exchange Rate API.
- What data do you keep? Your name, email, university, arrival date and the status you enter. Never passport or Emirates ID numbers, never documents.
- Is Wayfare a government service? No. It links to official sources such as ICP, RTA and Dubai Land Department.
- Which universities are on Wayfare? Heriot-Watt University Dubai is the first.

## 6. SEO / AEO
Index it (evergreen offer, search intent matches). Title: "Wayfare: feel at home in Dubai before you land". Meta description: the hero sub. FAQ rendered as plain Q&A with FAQPage JSON-LD. og:image made from the brand. Share, admin and app routes send noindex.
